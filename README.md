Retail-Sales-Profit-Analysis

Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **retail-sales-profit-analysis** built using **Power BI**. The objective is to analyze sales trends, profitability drivers, regional performance, category contribution, and the impact of discounting to support business decision-making.
The dashboard is designed to reflect **real-world business analysis**, focusing not only on revenue but also on margin optimization and loss identification.


Business Objectives

* Evaluate overall sales and profit performance
* Identify high- and low-performing regions
* Analyze category-wise profit contribution
* Understand the impact of discounting on profitability
* Identify loss-making products
* Provide actionable business recommendations


Tools & Technologies

**Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling & visualization
**Excel / CSV** – Data cleaning, Real time Data from Kaggle
**DAX** – Measures and KPIs


Key KPIs

**Total Sales:** ₹2.30M
**Total Profit:** ₹286.40K
**Profit Margin:** ~12%

These KPIs provide a high-level view of business health and profitability.


Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales & Profit Trend (Monthly)

* Compares monthly sales growth against profit trends
* Highlights periods where sales growth does not translate into profit

### 2. Regional Profit Performance

* Identifies profit contribution by region
* Highlights underperforming regions despite strong sales

### 3. Category-wise Profit Contribution

* Compares profitability across product categories
* Clearly shows loss-generating categories

### 4. Discount Impact on Profitability

* Demonstrates how higher discount levels reduce profit margins
* Identifies discount thresholds that lead to losses

### 5. Top 10 Loss-Making Products

* Identifies products contributing most to total losses
* Helps prioritize product-level corrective actions

### 6. Interactive Filters

* Region and Category slicers for focused analysis


Key Business Insights

* Central region shows strong sales but weaker profitability
* Furniture category consistently contributes to losses
* Higher discount levels significantly reduce profit margins
* A small set of products accounts for most total losses


Recommendations

* Introduce discount approval limits for high-discount orders
* Re-evaluate pricing and cost structure for the Furniture category
* Conduct regional cost and pricing review for Central region
* Focus on high-margin products and rationalize loss-making SKUs
